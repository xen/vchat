## Privacy policy

Read our privacy policy. What you need to know about your personal information:

- We collect only the information that you provide to us in the registration form (name, surname, email, phone).
- Some additional information may be collected unintentionally to ensure the technical operation of the site (IP addresses, browser versions, operating systems).
- We use cookies to authorize users.
- We may have access to payment data from partners providing processing and may transfer to them some of the personal data necessary for making a payment.
- We may use social network pixels for advertising targeting.

### Collection and use of personal information

Personal information is data that allows you to identify a person or contact them. We process only the information that was transferred to us by visitors and store it in a secure form and regulate access to it for employees and partners of the site. Some of this information may lead to the disclosure of personal information about people mentioned on the site. By using the services of the site, you agree that the data you have provided may be disclosed publicly and transferred to third parties (banks and payment systems, law enforcement agencies upon official requests).

### What information about users do we collect

Personal information may include:

- User's first and last name
- Email address
- Contact phone number
- Payment information
- Links to profiles in social networks
- Other information provided by the user explicitly or implicitly

When creating an account on the site, we offer to fill out a questionnaire with information that allows us to provide personalized information to users. We can publish this information publicly with the consent of the user.

We can periodically use this information to inform about new projects or updates to the service or related projects.

In certain cases, we have the right to ask you to provide government-issued identification data or a legal document to confirm the right to manage the information published on the site. Examples of cases when this may happen:

- We suspect that you have used the wrong payment information.
- There is a dispute related to a change in account access rights or suspicious activity.
- A request from a payment system.
- Information necessary to terminate a contract.

We also collect data that does not directly relate to a specific person. We may collect, use, transfer and disclose such information for any purpose.

We may collect such information as the operating system version and language, unique device identifier, referring URL, location and time zone.

### Cookies and other technologies

Our websites, emails and advertisements placed on the website may use cookies, in particular pixel tags and other technologies. These technologies allow us to better understand user behavior, learn which pages of our websites they visit, and prioritize the development of new services. Information collected using cookies is divided into two types: information required for the technical operation of the site and statistical information. Statistical information is not considered personal, and we store it in aggregate form.

Without using cookies, the functionality of the site is not guaranteed.

We automatically collect certain information and store it in log files. Such information includes:

- IP address
- Information about the browser, operating system, language
- Date and time stamps
- Information about visits

We may use click tracking tools in email messages.

By using our products and projects, you assume responsibility for the fact that some information about your actions may be collected by third parties. For example, your provider can track which IP address the request was made to. For our part, we notify you that we may be forced to disclose information by a court decision. In order to reduce the likelihood of such an outcome, we place our servers in jurisdictions that protect the rights and personal information of users. And we strictly monitor the implementation of the GDPR, some provisions of the laws of countries "On the protection of personal data".

We may be required to disclose your personal information to comply with the law, legal process, litigation, or requests from government authorities in your country of residence or other countries. We may also disclose your personal information if it is necessary for national security, law enforcement, or other purposes of public importance.

We may also disclose your personal information if necessary to enforce our terms or protect our operations or users.

### Privacy Policy

We treat personal information with care and responsibility and protect all communications with our services via an encrypted connection. We use servers with limited access to store data.

Our sites may contain links to third-party websites, products, and services. In this case, we recommend that you review the privacy policies of such third parties.
